A closer look at your browsing.

Time on websites
Explore time spent on websites, with charts and period filters for your browsing statistics.
Network activity
See received-data measurements for the active tab on the toolbar and in your session statistics. This is not a meter for all browser or device traffic.
Connection tests
Run a test when you need an estimate of latency, jitter, download and upload speed. Tests contact Cloudflare and transfer test data.
A panel that stays
Keep the interface open while switching websites. Choose your language and appearance; resize the panel using Chrome.

Your data, explained plainly.

WebTracker sends completed browsing sessions, including full visited URLs, domains, timestamps, durations and measured received bytes, to Supabase to provide your statistics.

Each installation receives a generated identifier and technical account. This is pseudonymous, not anonymous: URLs can identify you, and database administrators can access stored records.

Chrome displays a debugging notification while the extension measures active-tab traffic. Restricted browser pages are excluded.
